About WildFIndia

A student project for discovering India's wildlife and building a personal nature journal.

Explore, identify, collect

Browse {{ species_count }} researched species profiles and {{ park_count }} protected-area directory entries. Upload a wildlife photo, check the model's suggestions, and confirm a matching species to add it to your private WildDex.

AI results are suggestions. WildDex records your confirmation; it is not a scientific verification of a sighting.

Your data

Your journal, observations, and identification history belong to your signed-in account. Journal notes are private. Submitted park reviews become public only after moderation.

You can export your journal, remove a discovery, and delete an identification photo from your history. Deleting a photo does not remove an already confirmed WildDex species.

Sources and coverage

The protected-area directory uses the source list bundled with this project. It is a dated catalogue, not a live official register. Park introductions link to their source articles. State ranges are representative and incomplete. Check the local Forest Department before planning a visit.

The map uses approximate display points. A park only lists species explicitly linked in our catalogue. Photographs and introductions are cached locally so catalogue pages do not wait for another website.

Animal and park illustrations use Twemoji graphics under CC BY 4.0. Map boundaries are credited on the map page.
